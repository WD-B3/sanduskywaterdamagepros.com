Emergency Basement Water Damage in Sandusky, OH

A flooded basement can hold power panels, a furnace and a water heater. Stay out of the water, shut off power safely and call the 24/7 line.

Call (567) 406-9153

A wet basement is a different job from a wet kitchen. Basement water damage cleanup in Sandusky, OH deals with a below-grade room that often holds the electrical panel, the furnace and the water heater. If water is standing or rising, call the 24/7 line and stay away from anything electrical.

While you wait, turn off the main water valve if a pipe is the source, move items to higher ground and keep out of the water. If the panel is in the basement and water is near it, do not go downstairs to flip breakers. Call the electric utility or 911.

When basement water is an emergency

Call at once for any of these: water over a few inches deep, water still rising, water reaching the panel, furnace or water heater, a failed or overloaded sump pump, drains that bubble or overflow or any smell of sewage.

Basement electrical safety

Water and electricity do not mix. Never step into standing water near a panel, outlet or appliance. If you are not sure the power is off, assume it is on. Call the electric utility or 911 for guidance before you call restoration.

Restoration is not waterproofing

This is worth saying plainly. Restoration removes water and dries the structure. It does not seal a foundation or fix drainage. If your basement floods repeatedly, a waterproofing or foundation specialist can look at the cause. The restoration crew can tell you what they see, but will not promise the water will not return.

How a flooded basement is handled

Water comes out first with pumps and extractors, as described in water extraction. The crew then removes wet carpet, pad and the lower part of drywall if needed and checks for hidden moisture behind finished walls.

Drying follows the approach in structural drying and dehumidification, with extra attention to concrete and framing, which dry slowly. Contaminated water follows sewage backup cleanup. Carpeted basement floors are covered in carpet water damage restoration.

A note on Sandusky and Lake Erie

Sandusky sits on Lake Erie at the mouth of Sandusky Bay, and much of the city is low ground near the water. Whether your basement is at risk depends on your lot, the grading and the drainage, not on a general rule. Check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask the City of Sandusky building division about local requirements.

Below-grade drying and water classification follow ANSI/IICRC S500, the published industry standard. Ask whether the crew works to it.
