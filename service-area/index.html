Water Damage Service Area: Sandusky and Nearby Erie County Towns

The 24/7 line covers Sandusky, Huron and the surrounding Erie County area. Here is what that means on the ground.

Call (567) 406-9153

The dispatch line is centered on Sandusky, OH and reaches nearby Erie County towns. Sandusky is the Erie County seat on Lake Erie, and the area includes the bay shore, the river mouth and inland neighborhoods. This page lists where crews work, which ZIP codes belong to each place and which local utilities and offices to know before an emergency.

Sandusky, Ohio

Sandusky is served by ZIP codes 44870 and 44871. The City of Sandusky runs its own water through Big Island Water Works, and its Building Division follows the State of Ohio building, plumbing and mechanical codes. For restoration, that means the main water valve inside your home is yours, the public line before the meter is the city’s and permits for rebuild work go through the building division.

Within the city, the original Kilbourne Plat, the downtown blocks, Battery Park and the Coal Docks area on the waterfront share the same dispatch line. So do the East Side, South Side and West Side areas that the city’s transit system uses to describe the city. Properties near the bay and the Sandusky River should check the FEMA flood map for their address.

Huron, Ohio

Huron lies east of Sandusky along US 6 and OH 2 and uses ZIP code 44839. It sits in Erie County, not Huron County, so county and sewer contacts differ from what a search for the name might suggest. The full details sit on the water damage restoration Huron page.

Other places we take calls from

Milan and Castalia are covered through the Huron area, and the lakeshore communities of Port Clinton, Kelleys Island, Put-in-Bay and Middle Bass can also call. Island jobs depend on ferry and travel schedules, so describe where you are and the dispatcher explains what is possible.

What to know before you call from outside Sandusky

Utilities and permit offices differ by town. If a leak is on the public side of the meter, call the water provider for your address. If the problem is inside your property, the 24/7 line is the right call. The services index helps you pick the page that matches your situation, and water extraction is the usual first step for standing water.

Business owners can also read about commercial water damage restoration, and homeowners who are worried about a heavy storm can read storm water damage cleanup before bad weather arrives.

How the line works across towns

There is one dispatch line for every town on this page. You do not need to choose a number by location. Say your town and ZIP code, describe the damage and the dispatcher explains what is possible. Distance, weather and traffic change how quickly a crew can reach you, so you hear an update on the call rather than a fixed promise.

Questions to settle before a loss

  • Which provider supplies water to your address, and what is its emergency number?
  • Where is your main shutoff, and does everyone in the house know?
  • Is your property near the lake, bay or river, and what does the FEMA flood map show?
  • Does your policy include water backup or flood coverage?
  • Which office handles building permits for your town?

Answering these before water arrives saves time on the worst day. The homeowner guides go through several of them in detail.

Neighborhoods in Sandusky with their own pages

Where there is solid local information, a neighborhood gets its own page. Downtown Sandusky covers the original plat, storefront blocks with apartments above and newer bayfront homes. Battery Park covers the waterfront site at the eastern edge of downtown and its redevelopment. The East Side covers the established residential streets east of downtown.

Other parts of the city, including the West Side and the South Side, are served on the same line but do not have separate pages, because no reliable local source was found to say anything specific about them beyond what the Sandusky sections above already cover.
