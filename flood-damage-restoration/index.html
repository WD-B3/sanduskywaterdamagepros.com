Emergency Flood Damage Cleanup in Sandusky, OH

Flood water from rain, the bay or the ground is not clean water. Stay out of it, shut off power safely and call the 24/7 line.

Call (567) 406-9153

Flood damage cleanup in Sandusky, OH is more than removing water. Flood water arrives from outside the plumbing system, so it can carry soil, bacteria and chemicals into the building. The first priority is safety, the second is speed, and both start with a call to the 24/7 line.

While you wait, stay out of standing water if you can, switch off power only from a dry panel, and move valuables to higher ground. Photograph the water line on walls before anything is moved, because the height matters for your insurance conversation.

When flood water is an emergency

Call at once if any of these are true. Water is still entering the house. It is more than a few inches deep. It looks dark, muddy or smells like sewage. It has reached outlets, a furnace or a water heater. Anyone in the home has health conditions, is pregnant or is very young.

Sandusky sits on Lake Erie at the mouth of Sandusky Bay, and the Sandusky River runs into the bay near the city. The National Weather Service in Cleveland documents major regional flooding events, including the June 2006 flash floods, when Erie County was among six Ohio counties declared major disaster areas. Your own flood risk depends on your address, so check the FEMA flood map.

What the cleanup involves

Crews wear boots, gloves and respirators when working in contaminated water. Water comes out through pumps and extractors. Carpet, pad, insulation and drywall that soaked in Category 3 water are usually removed, since they cannot be reliably cleaned.

What stays gets cleaned, treated and dried with air movers and dehumidifiers, following the same approach as structural drying. If the flood entered from below, see basement water damage. If it came with a heavy storm, the storm water damage page covers wind and rain entry through the roof and walls.

Flood water and electricity

Do not walk through water that may touch outlets, cords or a panel. If the basement is flooded and the panel is down there, stay upstairs and call the electric utility or 911 for guidance. Do not stand in water to flip a breaker.

Gas appliances such as a water heater or furnace that sat in flood water need to be checked by a qualified technician before use.

Insurance and flood water: read this before you call your insurer

Most homeowners policies cover sudden plumbing failures but exclude flood. The Ohio Department of Insurance advises that flood coverage is bought separately and usually has a waiting period. Your policy language decides what counts as flood and what counts as other water, so read it or call your agent.

Water category definitions come from ANSI/IICRC S500, the published industry standard. Ask whether the company you hire works to it.

What shapes the cost of flood damage cleanup

Flood work is priced by the contamination level and the amount of material that has to be removed.
