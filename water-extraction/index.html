This is emergency water extraction in Sandusky, OH, and the clock matters. Water on a floor spreads sideways into baseboards, down into subfloors and up into drywall by wicking. Our 24/7 line answers at any hour, so call first and read the rest while the crew is being arranged.

While you wait, stop the source if you can reach the shutoff valve, then keep people and pets out of any room where water touches outlets, cords or power strips. Lift small valuables onto counters and pull rugs off wet floors. Do not run a household vacuum or any plugged-in tool in standing water.

When standing water is an emergency

Not every wet floor needs a pump, but these situations should not wait for morning:

  • Water covering a floor and reaching baseboards in any room
  • Water in a basement that is still rising or has already soaked carpet
  • Water near outlets, a panel, a furnace or a water heater
  • Dark, cloudy or foul-smelling water, which points to sewage backup or flood contamination
  • Water that has been sitting for a day or more, because wet materials give mold the moisture it needs

EPA guidance on mold and moisture says water-damaged areas should be dried within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth. Extraction is the first and fastest part of meeting that window.

Electrical and gas safety comes before the water

If water touches an outlet, panel or appliance, do not wade in. Cut power at the breaker only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground. If you cannot, call the electric utility or 911 before you call us.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 from outside. Water extraction can wait until the building is safe.

How extraction removes the water

The method depends on depth and surface. Deep water goes first with a submersible pump, then the floor is worked with extractors that pull water out of carpet and pad. Hard floors get squeegees and a dry vacuum so nothing is pushed into seams.

What comes after the water is gone

Extraction removes the water you can see. It does not dry the building. Once the standing water is out, moisture readings guide a drying plan with air movers and dehumidifiers, which is covered under structural drying and dehumidification.

If the water came from a failed supply line, the burst pipe page explains the repair sequence. If it rose from the ground or a storm, the flood damage page covers contaminated water, and soaked flooring is handled in carpet water damage.

Where Sandusky water usually goes

Homes close to Sandusky Bay and the Sandusky River sit on low ground, and a heavy rain can push water into a basement through a window well or floor drain. Check your own situation against the FEMA flood map for your address, and ask the city building division what applies to your block.

Inside the house, water follows gravity and then the building. A leak on the second floor often shows up first as a stain on the ceiling below, so a wet ceiling can mean there is water to extract on the floor above. Crews check both levels before they call a room dry.

What shapes the cost of water extraction

On insurance, sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowners policy, while slow leaks and neglect often are not. Flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and take photos before anything is moved.
